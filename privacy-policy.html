Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Lost & Found Portal. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use our application and website.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use the Lost & Found Portal, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address used for account registration.
  • Name provided in your profile.
  • College ID provided in your profile.
  • Phone number provided in your profile.
  • Information about lost or found items.
  • Item name, category, location, date and description.
  • Contact information provided with a report.
  • Photos uploaded with lost or found item reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used to provide and operate the Lost & Found Portal.

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To allow users to report lost items.
  • To allow users to report found items.
  • To display lost and found reports.
  • To allow users to search for reported items.
  • To allow users to manage their own reports.
  • To allow users to update their profile information.

3. Data Storage

Lost & Found Portal uses Firebase services provided by Google to provide authentication and database functionality. User account and application data may be stored using Firebase services.

4. Firebase Services

We use Firebase Authentication for user account authentication and Cloud Firestore for application data storage.

Firebase processes information according to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the application, such as Firebase services.

Information submitted in a lost or found report may be visible to authenticated users of the portal because the purpose of the application is to help users identify and recover lost or found items.

6. Data Security

We use appropriate technical measures to help protect information stored by the application. Access to application data is controlled using authentication and database security rules.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Data, Account Deletion and Data Retention

Users can manage their profile information and their own lost or found reports through the application.

Users can delete their own lost and found reports through the My Reports section.

Users can also permanently delete their account through the Account section of the application by selecting Delete Account and confirming their password.

When an account is deleted, the user's Firebase Authentication account, profile information stored in the application, and lost or found reports associated with that account are deleted.

We retain user information only for as long as it is needed to provide the Lost & Found Portal service or as required for legitimate operational or legal purposes. When a user deletes their account, the associated account information and reports are deleted as described above.

If you have difficulty deleting your account or have a request regarding your personal information, you can contact us at lostandfoundportal.in@gmail.com .

8. Children's Privacy

The application is intended for general users of the college lost and found service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the operation of the service.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the Lost & Found Portal, please contact us at:

Email: lostandfoundportal.in@gmail.com
